## Remaining acceptance gates and policy boundary

- **Live Google acceptance is open.** No real development Google credentials/callback were available. Qualified simulated provider hooks and synthetic real-API browser sessions are distinct from live provider acceptance.
- Ticket 6.1 owns Music numeric owner deletion/recomposition and socket disconnection. The worker refuses to mark Music-mapped deletion complete before that boundary. A post-6.1 integration must verify Music deletion and terminal canonical continuation together.
- The authoritative plan specifies phase-based cancellation but no timed deletion grace. The worker claims pending operations on its next scheduled pass (one minute); recovery is allowed while pending and denied once physical finalization begins. A fixed product grace would need an explicit policy decision, UX text and matching job/tests before production acceptance. The separate 30-day feedback retention policy is not a deletion grace.
- The schema's five-minute recovery-proof validity is enforced. Terminal finalization revokes retained proofs. Physical expiry purge of old proof rows after 24 hours is not implemented in this worker because the current runtime grant lacks DELETE on that table; this remains a retention gap for the owning 2.1 schema/authorization boundary and must be resolved before production acceptance.
